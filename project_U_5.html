Temperature Controlled
Automated Charcoal Stove

Project Details

Role: Group

1. Project Overview

This project delivers a sustainable and cost-effective cooking solution designed to address the energy crisis, offering functionality comparable to electric cookers.

2. Key Features & Implementation

System Functionality

  • Automated Control: Engineered with precise temperature controlling and timing mechanisms to ensure consistent cooking results.
  • Versatility: Designed to support multiple cooking methods with even heat distribution, overcoming common limitations of traditional charcoal stoves.

Custom Hardware & Electronics

  • PCB & Circuit Design: Custom-developed circuit and PCB designs to manage the automated features and logic.
  • Power Management: Integrated a rechargeable battery pack to ensure uninterrupted usage during power outages and enhance portability.
  • User Interface: Features LED indicators for visual mode confirmation and a dedicated power button for ease of use.

Design & Enclosure

  • Enclosure Design: Designed a custom enclosure to house the components safely and provide a user-friendly form factor.
